Mobile Money Scams

Fraudsters may pretend to be agents, relatives or service providers. They may ask you to reveal your PIN or send money.

Protection:

Never reveal your PIN. Confirm transactions using official channels.

💼

Fake Job Offers

Scammers may promise high salaries and guaranteed jobs in exchange for registration fees.

Protection:

Verify the employer, company address and official recruitment process before paying.

❤️

Romance Scams

Someone you meet online may build trust and later request money or financial help.

Protection:

Do not send money to people you have not properly verified.

🔗

Phishing Links

Fake links may imitate banks, delivery companies or official websites to steal your information.

Protection:

Avoid unknown links. Check the website address before entering personal details.
