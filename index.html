Common Scams in Ghana

📱 Mobile Money Fraud

Fake payment messages, false transfers and requests for your PIN.

💼 Fake Job Offers

Fraudsters promise employment after collecting registration fees.

❤️ Romance Scams

People create fake relationships to request money.

🛒 Online Shopping Fraud

Fake sellers collect payments without delivering products.

💰 Investment Scams

Promises of guaranteed profits and unrealistic returns.

🎁 Fake Prize Scams

Messages claiming you have won a prize but must pay first.

🔗 Phishing

Fake websites and links designed to steal personal information.

📱 Mobile Money Protection

Protect Your MoMo Account

  • Never share your Mobile Money PIN.
  • Never share your OTP verification code.
  • Check your actual balance before trusting payment messages.
  • Do not approve unknown transactions.
  • Never click suspicious payment links.
  • Contact your network provider through official channels.
IMPORTANT: Your PIN and OTP belong only to you. Never share them.

🛡️ Digital Safety Tips

1. Strong Passwords

Use strong and unique passwords for your accounts.

2. Two-Factor Authentication

Activate additional security on important accounts.

3. Verify Websites

Check website addresses before entering personal information.

4. Avoid Unknown Links

Do not click suspicious links from unknown senders.

5. Protect Personal Information

Never share your PIN, password or OTP.

6. Verify Job Offers

Research companies before paying recruitment fees.

7. Update Your Phone

Install security updates and use trusted applications.
