Common Scams in Ghana

  • Mobile Money scams: Fake payment messages and calls pretending to be MoMo agents.
  • Fake job offers: People asking for registration fees before giving you a job.
  • Online shopping scams: Sellers collecting money without delivering goods.
  • Romance scams: Strangers asking for money after building online relationships.
  • Fake investment scams: Promises of guaranteed high profits.

How to Protect Yourself

  • Never share your MoMo PIN with anyone.
  • Always verify payments before sending goods.
  • Never click on suspicious links.
  • Do not pay money to get a job.
  • Contact your bank if you suspect fraud.

Safety Tips

  • Never share your MoMo PIN or password.
  • Never share your OTP verification code.
  • Check website addresses before entering personal details.
  • Do not send money to strangers promising jobs or prizes.
  • Use strong and different passwords.
  • Verify information before sending money.

MoMo Scam Protection

  • Never give anyone your MoMo PIN.
  • Check your actual account balance before confirming a payment.
  • Do not trust screenshots as proof of payment.
  • Never follow instructions from suspicious callers.
  • Contact your mobile money provider through its official channels if you suspect fraud.
