Learn About Scams

📱 Mobile Money Scams

Scammers may pretend to be Mobile Money agents or send fake payment messages to trick you.

How to protect yourself:

  • Check your actual account balance before trusting a payment message.
  • Never share your PIN or secret codes.
  • Contact your mobile network using its official support channel.

💼 Fake Job Offers

Fraudsters may promise high salaries or jobs abroad and demand money before giving you a job.

How to protect yourself:

  • Verify the company and its official contact details.
  • Be careful when someone demands a registration fee urgently.
  • Do not send your passport or personal documents to unknown people.

🛒 Online Shopping Scams

Fake sellers may advertise products online, collect payment and disappear.

How to protect yourself:

  • Check seller reviews and independent information.
  • Be suspicious of prices that are too good to be true.
  • Use safer payment methods when available.

📈 Investment Scams

Some scammers promise guaranteed profits or unusually high returns.

How to protect yourself:

  • Avoid investments promising guaranteed profits.
  • Research the company before sending money.
  • Never invest money you cannot afford to lose.

Important Safety Tips

🔐 Protect Your Information

Keep your passwords, PINs and verification codes private.

📩 Check Suspicious Messages

Do not click unknown links or download suspicious attachments.

💰 Think Before Paying

Verify requests for money before sending funds to anyone.

📞 Ask for Help

Contact your bank, mobile network or the appropriate authorities through official channels if you suspect fraud.
