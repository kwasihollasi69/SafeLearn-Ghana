🛡️ Learn about online safety

📱 Mobile Money Scams

Never share your MoMo PIN or OTP with anyone. Always verify payment messages before sending money.

💼 Fake Job Offers

Be careful when someone asks you to pay money to get a job. Verify the company and its official contact details.

🔗 Fake Links

Do not click suspicious links. Check the website address before entering your personal information.

🔐 Password Safety

Use strong, unique passwords. Never share your passwords with strangers.

⚠️ Remember:

Never send money or share secret codes because of an urgent message. Stop and verify first.
