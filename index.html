Learn how to identify scams and protect your money and personal information.

🚨 Common Scams

  • Fake job offers asking for registration fees.
  • Mobile Money fraud messages.
  • Fake investment promises.
  • Fake online shopping sellers.

🛡️ Safety Tips

  • Never share your Mobile Money PIN.
  • Never share your OTP code.
  • Verify a job offer before paying.
  • Do not click suspicious links.
